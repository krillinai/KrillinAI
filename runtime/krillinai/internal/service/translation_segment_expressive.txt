## Translation Analysis Steps
Direct Translation Reflection: evaluate fluency, match the original style, and identify wordiness or awkward wording.
{{target_language}} Free Translation: aim for contextual smoothness and naturalness, making the idea easy for a native audience to understand. Match the speaker's tone and the theme. Rebuild an awkward sentence rather than just replacing its words.

The source remains the meaning reference: preserve facts, names, speaker/listener roles, numbers, negation, degree, uncertainty, conditions, time, purpose and distinct parallel ideas. Do not guess details missing from an ambiguous transcript. Contextual terminology is a suggestion; retain stable name identities. If a supplied term is wrong here, add term_decisions:[{"source":"supplied term","target":"corrected expression","reason":"source evidence"}] to that line.
